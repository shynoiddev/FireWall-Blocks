- Network Monitoring Mode: Added a real-time traffic sniffer via the local VPN to intercept and view which apps are attempting to connect to the internet, allowing direct blocking from the log screen.
- App Status Color-Coding: Visually differentiate apps in the list with specific colors: Red for System apps, Yellow for Disabled apps, and Grey with a strike-through for Uninstalled apps that still have orphan firewall rules.
- Orphaned Rules Management: The app now scans for and displays previously uninstalled apps that still have leftover firewall rules in storage, allowing you to clean them up.
- Enhanced Sorting & Filtering: Expanded list management with new options to show blocked apps last, show disabled apps only, or filter by uninstalled apps.
- UI & Performance Enhancements: Integrated fast scrolling for quicker list navigation, locked user inputs during app loads to prevent race conditions, and added smooth rotation handling to prevent screen resets.
- New confirmation dialog when copying rules from one another.
- in Shizuku mode, when copying VPN settings, the rules get bundled in UI correctly. (Shizuku limitations)
